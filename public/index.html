    System architecture / Built for real open play

    Designed around how open-play courts actually run

    Four core mechanics eliminate queue politics, uneven blowouts, and crowded organizer desks across community parks and indoor clubs.

    01.Fairness-First Matchmaking

    The longest-waiting player is mathematically locked into the next open court so nobody gets skipped. The remaining spots are selected from a tight look-ahead window to minimize team rating gaps, repeat partners, and back-to-back rematches.

    Factor evaluated per match Priority rule Relative weight
    Longest-waiting player (#1) Always included in next match Guaranteed
    Team skill balance (DUPR delta) Even combined team ratings 10.0×
    Repeat partner avoidance Fresh partners every rotation 8.0×
    Repeat opponent variety Minimizes immediate rematches 2.5×

    02.PX Live Board & Smart ETA

    Display a venue QR code once. Players tap their name on their phone to see their live wait estimate—and receive a chime and vibration the moment they are called. Append &tv=1 for a clubhouse big-screen board.

    03.Fixed Partners & Co-Organizer Sync

    Lock two players together for doubles so they move through the queue as a unit and always share the same side of the net. Share a club code so multiple organizers manage different courts simultaneously in real time.

    Unified partner pairs Locked duos stay paired across balanced rotations and Winners Stay streaks.
    Instant club codes Co-organizers join with a short code—every score syncs live across phones.

    04.Built for Courts With Weak Cell Signal

    Public parks rarely have reliable Wi-Fi at court 6. Pickle X Matcher is engineered as a local-first progressive web app: every check-in, court assignment, and score writes immediately to device storage and catches up automatically whenever connectivity returns.

    One-tap Undo everywhere Entered a score on the wrong court? Reverse any match or roster edit in a single tap.

    Session leaderboards Automatic win-loss records, point differentials, and game logs derived from verified results.
    Portable JSON backups Export or import your full club roster and session history anytime with zero lock-in.
